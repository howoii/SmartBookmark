# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Smart Bookmark serves Chrome users who save, revisit, and organize many pages across work, research, and daily browsing. They use it in short, context-heavy moments: saving the current tab, searching for a known page, cleaning a tag, moving a bookmark, or checking sync/settings state without leaving the browser workflow.

## Product Purpose

Smart Bookmark is an AI-assisted bookmark manager for saving, organizing, and searching bookmarks with less manual upkeep. It combines extension-owned bookmark storage, Chrome bookmark integration, automatic tag/excerpt/embedding support, quick save, quick search, side panel management, settings, and WebDAV sync. Success means users can capture a page quickly, trust that the bookmark can be found later, and understand what the extension is doing without extra ceremony.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, efficient, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a compact browser tool: fast to scan, modest in presentation, explicit about state, and careful with user data. It should support repeated use rather than perform for attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid SaaS landing-page styling, oversized hero composition, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, playful illustration-led surfaces, one-note purple or dark-slate palettes, and card-heavy layouts that reduce information density. Avoid mystery AI behavior: suggestions, generated tags, semantic search, and sync state need practical copy and visible feedback.

## Design Principles

- Search-first utility: common actions should stay close to the search/save flow and remain keyboard-friendly.
- Dense but calm: show enough information to decide quickly without turning every item into a decorative card.
- Explicit state over magic: syncing, generation, preview search, selection, and errors should be visible and plain.
- Local control: destructive actions, browser bookmark writes, and sync behavior need clear boundaries and confirmation where risk exists.
- Consistent cross-context behavior: popup, quick save, quick search, and settings should share visual patterns even when their layouts differ.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve visible focus states, keyboard paths, semantic button/input roles, and screen-reader labels for icon-only controls. Do not communicate status by color alone; pair color with iconography, labels, or layout. Keep motion short and state-driven, and avoid animations that obstruct fast repeated workflows.
